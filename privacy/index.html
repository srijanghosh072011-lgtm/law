Privacy

What this site collects

Short version: nothing that identifies you, unless you choose to email me.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. There is nothing to consent to, which is why there is no cookie banner. If that ever changes, this page changes first and a banner appears with it.

Forms

There are no forms on this site. Every “get in touch” button opens your own email application with a message addressed to me — nothing is submitted to or stored by this website.

Fonts and third parties

Typefaces are served by Google Fonts, so your browser requests two font files from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Google receives the request as part of serving those files, and its own terms cover that. No other third party is loaded on this site: no tracking pixels, no embedded video, no chat widget.

Server logs

The hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs of requests, in the ordinary way that any web server does, for security and reliability. I do not connect those logs to individual people and I do not use them for marketing.

If you email me

I keep what you send — your name, your email address and whatever you tell me about your business — for as long as I am talking, and afterwards for as long as I need it for records of work done. I do not sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list.

Your rights, and how to use them

You can ask what I hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Ask via the contact page and I will action it. If a project is running, work done under an agreement may need to be kept for accounting purposes — I will tell you plainly if that applies.

Client projects

Work I build for clients is a separate matter from this website. The automations I build run inside the client's own accounts, under the client's own terms, and this policy does not cover them.
