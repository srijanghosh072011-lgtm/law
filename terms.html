The agreement.

Placing an order means you accept what is on this page. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

Orders

An order is an offer to buy. It is accepted when we email you to say the shirt has shipped — not when payment is taken. If we cannot fulfil an order we will cancel it and refund in full.

Prices are in pounds sterling and include UK VAT. Orders outside the UK are shown duties and import charges at checkout.

Descriptions

Shirts are listed by colourway — the colours, stripe geometry, collar and trim shown in the illustration on the product page. That illustration is what the shirt looks like. Screens vary, so exact colour rendition will too.

Measurements are given flat, in centimetres, and are accurate to within about a centimetre.

Personalisation

You can have a name of up to twelve characters and a squad number from 1 to 99 printed. We screen every submission and we refuse:

  • Slurs, hate terms, and anything targeting a person or group.
  • Sexual, violent or drug-related wording.
  • A rival club's name on a shirt it does not belong on.
  • A player's name on a colourway they are not associated with.
  • Anything we judge, reasonably, that we do not want to make.

The customiser flags these before you pay. If something slips through, we will contact you to change it, or cancel and refund the order. We do not print it and charge you for the argument.

Shipping

Unprinted shirts are picked and despatched the same working day where possible. Printed shirts are pressed and cured individually, which adds roughly seventy-two hours before despatch.

Everything ships tracked. Delivery estimates are shown at checkout and are estimates — once a parcel is with the carrier, its timing is theirs.

Returns

Unworn, unwashed shirts in their original packaging can be returned within fourteen days of delivery for a full refund. Start it by emailing the address below; return postage is yours unless the shirt is faulty or we sent the wrong one.

Personalised shirts cannot be returned or exchanged unless faulty. A name pressed into a shirt makes it unsellable to anyone else. This does not affect your statutory rights where an item is faulty or not as described.

Faulty shirts: tell us within thirty days and we will replace it or refund it, including postage both ways.
