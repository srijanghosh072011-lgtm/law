Questions worth asking

Mostly about trust, which is the right thing to be asking about.

How do you stop it making things up?

By narrowing what it is allowed to answer from. It reads your approved material rather than the open internet, it is told that “I do not know, I have passed this on” is a correct answer, and it hands over instead of filling a gap with something plausible. No assistant is perfect; one that is honest about its limits is safe to put in front of customers.

Can it see customer data?

Only what you decide it needs, and normally only the record of the person it is speaking to. Access is scoped deliberately and stays read-only unless there is a clear reason otherwise. Anything it should not touch is simply never connected.

What happens when it cannot help?

It hands over with the conversation, the customer record and what it already tried attached, so the person picking it up starts informed. A handover that loses context is worse than no assistant at all.

Is this just a chatbot?

A scripted chatbot follows a decision tree someone drew in advance, and falls over the moment a question leaves the tree. An assistant reads your actual documents and current data, so it can handle a question nobody predicted and stays correct when your prices change.
