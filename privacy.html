Privacy

What we hold, and why.

This describes what this site actually collects today. If that changes, this page changes with it.

The mailing list

If you enter an email address into the drop list, we store that address so we can email you when a colourway goes live. That is the only reason it exists. We do not sell it, rent it, or hand it to anyone for their own marketing.

Every email carries an unsubscribe link. Using it removes your address entirely — there is no suppressed-but-retained copy.

Orders

Placing an order means we hold the name, delivery address, email address and order contents needed to make and send it, plus whatever the printed name and number were. Payment is handled by our payment provider; card details are never sent to this site and we never see them.

Order records are kept for six years because tax law requires it.

Your browser

The customiser saves your bag in your own browser's local storage so it survives a refresh. It never leaves your device and we cannot read it. Clearing your site data removes it.

We do not set advertising cookies and we do not run third-party trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google receives the request for the font file, including your IP address, as it would for any hosted asset.

Rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask for a copy of what we hold on you, ask for it corrected, ask for it deleted, or object to how it is used. Email the address below and we will respond within one month.

If you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Contact

Data questions go to privacy@onze.store.
