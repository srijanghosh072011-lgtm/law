Questions

Before you ask.

How do I get the size right?

Every size on the customiser lists a flat chest measurement. Lay a shirt you already wear on a table, measure across the chest an inch below the armhole, and pick the number closest to it. If you are between two, take the larger — these are cut close through the body.

Can I have any name printed?

Twelve characters, letters and spaces, plus a number from 1 to 99. The customiser screens the wording as you type and will tell you straight away if it is not going on the shirt. That covers slurs and hate terms, and it covers the joke ones — a rival club's name, or a player on a shirt they never wore.

Why does personalisation take longer?

Printed shirts are set, pressed and cured individually, which adds up to about seventy-two hours before it joins the despatch queue. A shirt with nothing printed on it goes out the same day it is picked.

Can I return it?

Unworn shirts with the packaging intact can go back within fourteen days. Personalised shirts are the exception — once your name is pressed into it, nobody else is buying it — so check the preview before you confirm.

Do you ship outside the UK?

Yes, tracked, with duties calculated at checkout so nothing lands on you at the door. Delivery estimates are shown before payment.
